Callie the missing Linux calendar

A calendar Linux deserves.

Callie keeps up with your Google, Outlook and CalDAV calendars and gives you day, week, month and agenda views that are quick to read and quick to change. Free, open source, no subscription, and no AI features you did not ask for.

Download for Fedora or Debian Source on GitHub Support on Ko-fi

What it does

  • All your calendarsGoogle, Outlook and Microsoft 365, and CalDAV servers such as Fastmail, iCloud and Nextcloud, several accounts at once, synced in the background and readable offline. Show or hide each calendar with a click.
  • Calendars of your ownKeep calendars on your computer as files other apps can read, subscribe to calendars by their link, and add events from an .ics file to any calendar or save a calendar as one.
  • Four ways to lookDay, week, month and agenda views, with search and a keyboard for everything, vi keys included.
  • Quick to addType "Lunch with Alex tomorrow 12-1pm at Cafe Sol", or drag across the week.
  • Events your wayTimes, repeats down to "every 2 weeks on Monday and Thursday", places with search and maps, and guests from your contacts.
  • InvitationsAnswer for one event or the whole series, from the event or the tray of waiting invitations.
  • RemindersDesktop notifications with a button to join the video call, even when the link is in the notes, and a tray entry while Callie runs in the background.
  • Make it yoursSeven built-in themes, from Callie and Paper to Minimal and Tomorrow Night, or your own: change any color, import and export themes, base16 schemes included.
  • Where you workHome or Office, as you set it in Google Calendar, under each day's date.
  • A terminal, tooThe callie command lists, searches, adds and answers, with JSON for scripts.

Install

On Fedora 44 or later, or Debian 13 or later, this installs the latest release with your package manager, after checking the download, and adds Callie's repository so updates come with your system's: 

curl -fsSL https://callieapp.org/install.sh | sh

It stops without changing anything on other systems. To see what it would do first, add -s -- --dry-run after sh. Or download the package from the latest release and install it yourself: 

sudo dnf install ./callie-*.x86_64.rpm   # Fedora
sudo apt install ./callie_*_amd64.deb    # Debian

Or build it from source, as the README describes.

Support Callie

Callie is free, with no subscription and nothing to sell. If it makes your days easier, you can support its development with a coffee on Ko-fi.

What it does not do

Callie is a calendar. It does not do tasks, scheduling links, or AI. It has no account system and no servers: your calendar data goes between your computer and your calendar provider, and nowhere else, apart from places you type, which Callie can look up on OpenStreetMap.
