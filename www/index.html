Callie the missing Linux calendar

A calendar Linux deserves.

Callie keeps up with your Google calendars and gives you day, week, month and agenda views that are quick to read and quick to change. Free, open source, no subscription, and no AI features you did not ask for.

Download for Fedora or Debian Source on GitHub

What it does

  • All your Google calendarsSeveral accounts, synced in the background and readable offline. Show or hide each calendar with a click.
  • Four ways to lookDay, week, month and agenda views, with search and a keyboard for everything, vi keys included.
  • Quick to addType "Lunch with Alex tomorrow 12-1pm at Cafe Sol", or drag across the week.
  • Events your wayTimes, repeats down to "every 2 weeks on Monday and Thursday", places with search and maps, and guests from your contacts.
  • InvitationsAnswer for one event or the whole series, from the event or the tray of waiting invitations.
  • RemindersDesktop notifications with a button to join the video call, even when the link is in the notes, and a tray entry while Callie runs in the background.
  • Make it yoursCallie and Callie Light, or your own theme: change any color, import and export themes.
  • Where you workHome or Office, as you set it in Google Calendar, under each day's date.
  • A terminal, tooThe callie command lists, searches, adds and answers, with JSON for scripts.

Install

Download the package for your system from the latest release, then install it: 

sudo dnf install ./callie-*.x86_64.rpm   # Fedora
sudo apt install ./callie_*_amd64.deb    # Debian and Ubuntu

Or build it from source, as the README describes.

What it does not do

Callie is a calendar. It does not do tasks, scheduling links, or AI. It has no account system and no servers: your calendar data goes between your computer and your calendar provider, and nowhere else, apart from places you type, which Callie can look up on OpenStreetMap. Read the privacy policy for the details.
