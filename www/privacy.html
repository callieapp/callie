Callie the missing Linux calendar

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 September 2026

Summary

Callie is a desktop application that runs entirely on your computer. It has no servers, no accounts, and no analytics. It does not collect, transmit, or sell your data. Your calendar information travels between your computer and the calendar providers you choose to connect, and nowhere else.

What Callie accesses

When you connect a Google account, Callie requests access to your Google Calendar data so it can show and edit your events. When you connect a CalDAV account, Callie accesses the calendars on the server you specify. Callie requests the narrowest permissions that allow it to function. It also reads your Google Calendar display settings, such as the day your week starts on, once when you connect an account, so Callie can match them.

How that information is used

Calendar data is used only to display your calendar, create and modify events at your direction, and show reminders. Processing happens locally on your computer. Callie does not send your calendar data to us or to any third party.

Where information is stored

  • Calendar data is cached in a local database in your user directory, so the app works offline.
  • Authentication tokens are stored in your system keyring through the Secret Service API, not in plain text.
  • Nothing is stored on any server operated by Callie, because there is none.

Google API Limited Use disclosure

Callie's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Specifically, Callie does not transfer Google user data to third parties, does not use it for advertising, does not use it to build profiles, and does not allow humans to read it.

Removing access

You can disconnect an account inside Callie at any time, which deletes its cached data and its stored token from your keyring. You can also revoke Callie's access to your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Uninstalling Callie removes the local cache.

Children

Callie is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date. The history of this document is public in the Callie repository.

Contact

Questions can be sent to privacy@callieapp.org.
